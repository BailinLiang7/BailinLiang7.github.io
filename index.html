📝Publications & Patents

Generalized Fault-Tolerant Control for Multi-Wheel Corner-Module Vehicles via Reconfigurable MPC

Bailin Liang, Junnian Wang*, Zidong Zhou, Guoshun Cai, and Yong Wang*

ISA Transactions (JCR Q1; IF=6.6), under review.

This paper proposes a Generalized Fault-Tolerant Control (GFT-RMPC) framework for multi-wheel corner-module vehicles, enabling cross-platform adaptation to arbitrary chassis configurations (4W/6W and beyond). It combines automatic topology identification, actuator-health-aware MPC reconfiguration, and physical latent space optimization to maintain stability and tracking accuracy across multi-scenario failures. Hardware-in-the-Loop testing verifies real-time execution within 12.36ms, confirming the practical deployability and robustness.

Nonlinear Shimmy Dynamics Analysis of Highly Integrated In-Wheel Motor Driving Unit with Independent Kingpin Steering for Electric Vehicles

Bailin Liang, Junnian Wang*, Yitong Wang, Chunlin Zhang, Yinpu Qi, and Heping Ling

Nonlinear Dynamics (JCR Q1; IF=5.7), under review.

This work develops a nonlinear shimmy analysis framework for highly integrated in-wheel motor driving units (HIDUs) with independent kingpin steering. A coupled shimmy dynamics model of the HIDU is established, and linear stability analysis, complex-variable averaging (CX-A), and an eigenspace-projected shooting (EPS) method are employed to characterize Hopf bifurcations and stable-manifold boundaries. A fast parametric sensitivity analysis (FPSA) method is proposed, achieving high prediction accuracy and an overall computational speed-up of 48.44× over conventional simulation-based approaches. Finally, the energy flow (EF) method reveals distinct parameter-dependent shimmy suppression mechanisms from the complementary perspectives of energy distribution and closed-loop power balance, providing guidance for design-oriented parameter selection and active shimmy control.

🔬Current Research

Nonlinear Shimmy Dynamics Analysis and Learning-Enhanced Control for Shimmy Suppression in Multi-Wheel Corner-Module Vehicles

Generalized Shimmy Dynamics Modeling: Developed a generalized nonlinear shimmy model for multi-wheel corner-module vehicles, capturing the inertial coupling among wheel-shimmy motion, suspension roll, and wheel rotational oscillations, together with lateral tire relaxation, steering backlash, and dry friction. Its modular architecture enables rapid adaptation across different multi-wheel corner-module chassis configurations.

Nonlinear Shimmy Dynamics Analysis: Investigating how steering backlash, dry friction, and dynamic coupling across multiple subsystems reshape stability boundaries and give rise to complex shimmy responses characterized by periodicity, quasiperiodicity, slow amplitude modulation, multistability, and nonzero-mean oscillations.

Shimmy-Aware Neural-Residual DiffTune-MPC: Developing a hybrid prediction model that augments a physics-guided nominal model with a neural residual to capture unmodeled nonlinear dynamics and reduce prediction mismatch. A shimmy-aware DiffTune-MPC scheme tunes the inner MPC cost weights against a closed-loop outer objective that minimizes band-limited shimmy energy and promotes oscillation-envelope contraction, enabling coordinated drive-brake-steer control for shimmy suppression.

Certifiable Latent Interface for Multimodal Robot Control

Explore history-dependent latent state representations that retain task- and control-relevant information from multimodal observation histories while attenuating nuisance variations, perception noise, and irrelevant history. Develop latent-to-policy parameterizations with built-in closed-loop guarantees so that policies generated from admissible latent states remain feasible and safe when integrated with a stabilizing low-level controller and the physical plant. Explore online latent-space planning and reinforcement-learning-based policy optimization within certified admissible sets to improve task performance while preserving closed-loop guarantees.
