🛞Corner-Module Technology

A corner-module vehicle is a reconfigurable, over-actuated, and highly redundant vehicle architecture that can also serve as a versatile mobile robotic platform. Centered on in-wheel motors, it integrates drive, braking, steering, and suspension functions at each wheel end, enabling maneuvering modes beyond those of conventional vehicle architectures. This highly integrated configuration broadens the vehicle dynamics envelope, improves transmission efficiency, and enhances energy-saving potential.

🔬Research Experience

  • Apr 2025 - Present

    Nonlinear Shimmy Dynamics Analysis of Highly Integrated In-Wheel-Motor Driving Units

    Key Technologies for Highly Integrated In-Wheel-Motor Driving Units · National Key R&D Program of China (Grant No. 2024YFB2505100)

    • Main contributor to the sub-project Motion Characteristics Analysis and Configuration Design of Highly Integrated In-Wheel-Motor Driving Units.
    • Developed a high-fidelity 13-DOF MATLAB/Simulink shimmy dynamics model for HIDUs with independent kingpin steering to investigate nonlinear shimmy mechanisms and parameter sensitivity. Built an Adams Car multibody model for comparative validation and implemented the workflow as an analysis software tool, laying the foundation for generalized shimmy dynamics research on multi-wheel platforms.
    • Based on a simplified nonlinear shimmy model, proposed a nonlinear dynamics analysis framework combining complex variable averaging (CX-A) and eigenspace-projected shooting for bifurcation analysis. Developed an FPSA method achieving an overall 48.44× computational speed-up over conventional simulation-based approaches. Energy-flow analysis reveals parameter-dependent shimmy suppression mechanisms through energy distribution and closed-loop power balance.
  • Sept 2026 - Present

    Coordinated Shimmy Suppression in Multi-Wheel Corner-Module Vehicles via Neural-Residual DiffTune-MPC

    • Generalized Shimmy Dynamics Modeling: Developed a generalized nonlinear shimmy model for multi-wheel corner-module vehicles, capturing the inertial coupling among wheel-shimmy motion, suspension roll, and wheel rotational oscillations, together with lateral tire relaxation, steering backlash, and dry friction. Its modular architecture enables rapid adaptation across different multi-wheel corner-module chassis configurations.
    • Nonlinear Shimmy Dynamics Analysis: Investigating how steering backlash, dry friction, and dynamic coupling across multiple subsystems reshape stability boundaries and give rise to complex shimmy responses characterized by periodicity, quasiperiodicity, slow amplitude modulation, multistability, and nonzero-mean oscillations.
    • Shimmy-Aware Neural-Residual DiffTune-MPC: Developing a hybrid prediction model that augments a physics-guided nominal model with a neural residual to capture unmodeled nonlinear dynamics and reduce prediction mismatch. A shimmy-aware DiffTune-MPC scheme tunes the inner MPC cost weights against a closed-loop outer objective that minimizes band-limited shimmy energy and promotes oscillation-envelope contraction, enabling coordinated drive-brake-steer control for shimmy suppression.

  • Nov 2025 - Jul 2026

    Generalized Fault-Tolerant Control for Multi-Wheel Corner-Module Vehicles via Reconfigurable MPC

    • Proposed a generalized fault-tolerant control framework integrating automatic topology identification, health-aware MPC reconfiguration, and physical latent-space optimization. The framework enables automatic adaptation to arbitrary multi-axle chassis configurations, maintaining stability and tracking accuracy under diverse faults while improving computational efficiency.
    • Validated GFT-RMPC across 18 steering, driving, and composite fault scenarios, demonstrating superior stability and tracking performance over baseline methods for both 4-Wheel and 6-Wheel configurations. Hardware-in-the-loop tests further verify real-time execution within 12.36 ms, confirming its cross-configuration adaptability and practical deployability.
  • Oct 2025 - May 2026

    Architecture Design of Multi-Mode Power-Multiplexed Electric Wheel Corner Modules

    • Proposed novel power-multiplexing electric wheel corner-module architectures to reduce structural redundancy arising from function-stacking in conventional designs. By sharing a single power source through switchable transmission paths, these architectures support kingpin/omnidirectional dual-mode steering, land/air dual-mode mobility, and multi-mode driving-braking control.
  • Jan 2024 - Oct 2024

    Wheel-End Energy Autonomy for Distributed-Drive Electric Vehicles

    • Developed a distributed wheel-end supercapacitor-battery architecture that reduces energy-transfer losses and battery stress through localized energy management.
    • Completed topology selection and parameter matching, ADVISOR/Simulink modeling, power-split control, and regenerative-braking optimization. Further integrated onboard energy storage into corner modules to enable local energy recovery, storage, and reuse while providing wheel-end energy autonomy and energy redundancy.

  • Certifiable Latent Interface for Multimodal Robot Control Research interest
